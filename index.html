Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

The App does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos.

Stored only on your device: your best score, level progress, coins, unlocked or equipped cosmetics, sound and shake settings, tutorial state, and ad-related preferences such as a Remove Ads entitlement. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you delete the App.

2. Advertising

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (interstitial ads between runs and optional rewarded video ads). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS or AAID on Android), only if you allow tracking when the device asks;

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Analytics

The App uses PostHog to understand how the game is used and to improve it. Analytics may include anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers stored on your device, in-app events (for example level started or finished, shop actions, ad-related events, coins earned), and device or app technical information. See posthog.com/privacy.

4. Your choices and consent

Tracking (iOS): the App asks for permission before accessing your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalised.

EEA, UK and Switzerland: a consent form from Google lets you accept or reject personalised ads. You can reopen your privacy choices from the privacy button on the App's title screen, where shown.

Rewarded ads are optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing. A Remove Ads purchase, if offered, is intended to turn off interstitial ads; rewarded offers may still be available.

5. How information is used and shared

Information collected by Google and PostHog is used to show, limit, measure and improve ads, understand feature usage, and prevent fraud. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive any information that directly identifies you. If you make an in-app purchase, Apple or Google handles billing.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, or to opt out of personalised advertising. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests are handled through the controls above or directly with Google or PostHog. You can also email us and we will help.

8. Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the App. Data held by Google and PostHog is kept according to their retention policies. Network features use HTTPS where those services provide it.
